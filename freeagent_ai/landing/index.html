How to connect
your FreeAgent account.

  1. Link your FreeAgent account.

    Open FreeAgent AI in your assistant and follow the account-linking steps.

  2. Describe the task.

    Tell your assistant which task you need help with. Provide the client, dates and rates where needed.

  3. Approve and verify.

    Approve proposed accounting changes, then check what FreeAgent holds.
